# Paper Outline

**Working title:** *Deterministic query routing versus LLM query planning for
federated QA over heterogeneous graph engines*

**Framing** (from [`../audit-2026-08-03.md`](../audit-2026-08-03.md) §7, Path 1
of four, recommended): the contribution is the **measurement**, not the
mechanism. Mediator-wrapper federation is 25 years old and "templates rather than
free generation" is a named family in KBQA since 2015. What does not exist in the
literature is a controlled ablation of a query-template library against free-form
LLM query generation — a null result verified across seven search formulations.

This framing converts the project's biggest liability into its contribution.
Hand-written templates and regex intent classification read as unambitious where
a reviewer expected an agent; under this framing they are the **treatment
condition**.

## The argument, in order

1. Federated QA across security KGs in **separate engines with different query
   languages** is unclaimed territory (C-011) — but AgCyRAG partially scoops the
   architecture, so lead with the result, not the design.
2. Losing the hand-written templates costs **−0.3568** correctness; swapping in
   LLM planning costs **−0.1185**. The templates, not the planner, are where
   accuracy lives (C-003). **The strongest result available.**
3. The deterministic path is also **637× faster** (C-004).
4. Readability is not traded against accuracy once the narrator is bound to
   verified claims (C-005).
5. Failures are not hallucinations: 26 of 36 were planning or retrieval (C-016).
6. **And here is the price** — the coverage cliff on non-anchored questions
   (C-014). *Reporting the trade rather than a victory is what inoculates the
   paper against its own honest finding.*

## What blocks each venue tier

**Demo track (EACL, Sept 2026):** nothing blocks it. Demo tracks do not require
baselines. Sections 1–5 plus a screencast.

**Applied conference:** three hard blockers, all in
[`../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`](../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md):

* **Q3 / C-012** — the free-form text-to-Cypher/Gremlin baseline does not exist.
  Arm G is a generic-primitives control; **no LLM writes a query in any arm.**
  Without this the headline claim is unsupported.
* **Q1 / C-013** — no holdout. The catalogue was co-authored with the templates.
* **Q4 / C-014** — the coverage cliff is unmeasured, so §7 cannot be written.

**Archival:** add Q5 (merged-single-graph baseline) and Q2 (seeds, significance).
Not recommended on the current evidence.
