## D-NNN — <decision in one line, stated as the conclusion>

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Context:** What forced the choice. The constraint or observation that made this a decision rather than a default.
**Options:** Each one actually considered, with the reason it was plausible.
**Decision:** What was chosen.
**Consequences:** What this costs. Comparability lost, work now required, doors closed. A decision entry with no cost listed has not been thought through.
