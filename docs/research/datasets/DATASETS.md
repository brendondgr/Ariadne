# Datasets

Provenance, licence, version, and access for every dataset any experiment
touches. One card per dataset under [`cards/`](cards/).

**Nothing here is authored by this project except the question catalogue.** Every
technique, attack pattern, mitigation, group, and identifier originates in MITRE.
The full third-party attribution text is in [`NOTICE`](../../../NOTICE) at the
repository root, reproduced under licences that require MITRE's copyright
designation to travel with any copy.

## Release status

**Licensing is resolved.** [`LICENSE`](../../../LICENSE) (Apache-2.0) was added on
2026-08-18 and [`NOTICE`](../../../NOTICE) carries the MITRE attribution the
ATT&CK and CAPEC terms require. The blocker recorded here until 2026-08-31 —
*"there is no LICENSE file"* — is closed.

⚠️ **Two release constraints remain, and one of them is not fixable by us.**

| # | Constraint | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The identity catalog is funder-supplied under the challenge agreement and is not redistributable.** The card below says so | A reviewer cannot obtain the data behind `EXP-2026-08-011`, `-013`, `-015` (identity half) and `-016` — which is where the strongest transfer results in the record live. **A route exists and has not been taken:** publish the tenant *generator* and the schema shape rather than the catalog, since the instances are synthetic anyway |
