### `[C-SZLO]` — Szlobodnyik, *Analytics* (MDPI) 5(3):25 — **the one that killed a claim**

**Threat: critical, and it is peer-reviewed.** Gergely Szlobodnyik,
"Feedback-Guided Prompt Injection Defense in Retrieval-Augmented Text-to-Cypher
Generation." *Analytics* 5(3):25, 2026. DOI 10.3390/analytics5030025. Received
18 May, accepted 21 July, **published 28 July 2026 — four weeks before the
audit.** Open access, CC-BY, single author. ✅ READ by the auditor.

It contains, in one paper: a six-category text-to-Cypher prompt-injection
dataset from sixteen parameterised templates; **Unsafe Operation Rate** and
**Attack Success Rate**; a validator model that intercepts unsafe Cypher and
feeds corrections back; a jury-of-models faithful/malicious/diverged classifier;
five foundation models against six training-free defence baselines; and
— *reportedly* — an identity-provider-driven access-control layer limiting
execution to the authorised portion of the schema.

**Independently re-verified 2026-08-26.** Citation, date, author, venue, DOI and
open-access status confirmed via DOI resolution and Semantic Scholar. The six
categories match **verbatim**. The metrics, the validator and the model list are
confirmed. **The access-control layer is NOT confirmed** — mdpi.com and SSRN
both refuse our fetcher — and it is the single mechanism that maps onto our own
schema-boundary refusal. **Q23 exists to close that.** An SSRN version
(`abstract_id=5669662`) exists whose posting date is unknown; if it predates
28 July the "four weeks" framing is wrong and this was findable earlier.

**What survives for us, pending Q23:** security-taxonomy schemas, where the data
*is* attack description and the schema boundary *is* a classification boundary;
Gremlin, for which no adversarial probe suite was found; database-privilege-level
rather than prompt-and-validator-level enforcement, executed against a live
store; and analyst-authored rather than model-authored probes — which dovetails
with C-021's argument about who writes the test set.

### `[C-AGCY]` — AgCyRAG, RAGE-KG @ ISWC 2025 — **corrected**

**Threat: high.** Kabul Kurniawan, **Rayhan Firdaus Ardian**, Elmar Kiesling,
Andreas Ekelhart. "AgCyRAG: an Agentic Knowledge Graph based RAG Framework for
Automated Security Analysis." RAGE-KG 2025 @ ISWC 2025, Nara, Japan. CEUR-WS
**Vol-4079, pp. 132–144**, published 27 October 2025. **Non-archival workshop
paper** (15 of 21 accepted). ✅ READ. Code at `github.com/sepses/multi-agents-cykg-rag`.

**Four authors, Ardian second** — this file previously listed three in the wrong
order.

**Corrected description.** SPARQL access is predefined; **the Cypher agent
generates its query with an LLM**. There is no `SERVICE` clause, no query
decomposition and no cross-store join: a guardrail agent routes, a reflection
agent decides whether to fire the second store, and a synthesis agent merges
inside a prompt. The defensible description is **agent-level orchestration
across heterogeneous stores**, not federation.

**Our belief about evaluation was right and is stronger than we stated.** No
evaluation section, no metrics, no baselines, no split, **no numbers of any
kind**. §4.2 is three qualitative transcript walkthroughs. Their conclusion:
comprehensive evaluation is future work.

**Consequence.** The sentence *"first agentic system issuing both Cypher and
SPARQL against heterogeneous unmerged security stores"* is **occupied**. What is
not occupied is any of it being measured. **And note rank 15 of the scoop watch:
this group has now said in two consecutive papers that evaluation is future
work. That is our benchmark, unbuilt, by an active group. Watch RAGE-KG 2026.**

## The mechanism is not novel — and that is fine

These establish that "use templates rather than free generation" is a recognised
family, which is why the contribution must be the *measurement*, not the idea.

| Key | Work | How it differs from us |
|---|---|---|
| `[T-AQQU]` | Bast & Haussmann, CIKM 2015 | Three query templates covering almost all of Free917 and WebQuestions. Seminal, single-source, Freebase, no LLM anywhere. Establishes the family is 10+ years old. |
| `[T-TQA]` | Zheng et al., PVLDB 2018 | The most explicit "templates rather than semantic parsers" argument in the literature. Single KG; no cross-engine federation and no LLM comparison. |

## Constrained generation — the obvious reviewer objection

A reviewer will ask what *eliminating* generation adds over *constraining* it.
The answer has to be the coverage–reliability trade measured explicitly (C-014),
not asserted.

| Key | Work | How it differs from us |
|---|---|---|
| `[T-PIC]` | PICARD, EMNLP 2021 | Constrains decoding rather than replacing it. Spider 69.9→74.1; unusable predictions 12%→2%. The strongest form of the objection. |
| `[T-GCD]` | Geng et al., EMNLP 2023 | Grammar-constrained decoding without finetuning. Same objection, different mechanism. |
| `[T-GCR]` | GCR / KG-Trie, **ICML 2025** | Graph-constrained reasoning; WebQSP Hit 92.2. ⚠️ Frequently miscited as ICLR — it is ICML. |
| `[T-OZS]` | Ozsoy, preprint | **The best quantified evidence of the cost of constraining:** execution success 63.8→86.7% while empty predictions rise 11→36. Directly supports framing the paper around a trade. |

### Added 2026-08-26 — the three artifacts in the gap

**How this differs from us — corrected wording.** Every entry above is flat
text, text-with-retrieval, or path strings. The general text-to-Cypher
benchmarks (CypherBench, SpCQL, SynthCypher, MindTheQuery, PIPE-Cypher,
KG2Cypher) have real gold Cypher and execution accuracy over Wikidata, film,
sport, finance and enterprise schemas — and **zero security-taxonomy content**,
confirming that half of the belief.

**Never write "no natural-language question resource exists over an ATT&CK
graph."** The audit's recommended and defensible wording:

> *No benchmark exists that pairs natural-language questions with executable
> gold queries in a standard graph query language, against a live graph store,
> scored by execution-based correctness, over MITRE ATT&CK, CAPEC, CVE, CWE,
> D3FEND or ATLAS.*

**D3FEND and ATLAS:** no question set of any kind, graph or flat, was located in
a search of web search, arXiv, CEUR-WS, ACL Anthology, ACM DL and Hugging Face
on 2026-08-26.

⚠️ **And note what we do not yet have.** That sentence describes a resource with
**gold queries**. Our banks carry a template *name*, seed identifiers and
computed gold *answers* — no executable query string. The gold query is
constructible from what we hold but is not emitted. **See C-025 and Q26; do not
write the sentence above about our artifact until Q26 is closed.**

**Two traps worth recording.**

AttackQA ships boolean `human_question` / `human_answer` columns, and the paper
reports a 20/50/30 split. `human_question` means *a human wrote the generator
script*. Filtering on it and describing the result as human-authored is a
defensible-looking citation that a reviewer reading §3.3 will catch.

CTIBench `cti-ate` is **four ATT&CK releases stale**. A direct read of ATT&CK
19.0 scores **0.607** precision against its gold — 466 identifiers where the
benchmark lists 283. Any `cti-ate` precision figure reported against 1.0
overstates its own headroom, ours included had we not measured the ceiling
(D-018).
