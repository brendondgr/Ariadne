## 4. Interpretation

**Completeness confirms the harness.** T3 `records_recall` for this arm is
0.9250 (EXP-2026-09-025) and the judge's completeness is 0.9187: the identifiers
the harness counts as retrieved are the ones the answer states. T4/T5 are
zero in the harness and near zero here; T6 is clean in both.

**What the judge adds that the harness cannot see.** The two fabricated
mappings and the "has no MITIGATED relationships … Found 4 item(s)" pattern
observed live in the workbench during Step 10 are the same defect: the
composer emits an absence claim from one sub-question beside an enumeration
from another. The harness scores the ids and never reads the sentence.

**What this pass cannot say.** Whether a widened answer (EXP-2026-09-024) or
a disclosed one (EXP-2026-09-028) reads better to an analyst than the
shipped answer. That needs the bridge to carry evidence records, and then
those two arms judged beside this one.

## 5. Threats to validity

- The bridge carries claims, abstentions and a record count, not records; every
  reading that depends on "evidence shown" is biased against the answer.
- The local judge, not the cross-model grader; the protocol says why.
- The run started before this folder existed; the protocol was written with
  no judgment read (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That the judge's hallucination flags at T3 (21 of 40) were almost all the
answer's *correct* identifiers, unsupported only because the bridge had not
shown the records that support them.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Carry evidence records through the bridge (the harness rows would need to
  keep them), then judge `expand_unbound` and `disclosed` beside `form_execute`.
- The composer's absence-claim-beside-enumeration wording: one defect, two
  fabricated-mapping verdicts and a visible contradiction in the workbench.
