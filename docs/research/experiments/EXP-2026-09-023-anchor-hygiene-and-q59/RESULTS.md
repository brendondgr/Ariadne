# RESULTS — EXP-2026-09-023 Anchor hygiene and Q59

## 1. Headline

**H1 (the 1 % frequency rule) was falsified and replaced by AMENDMENT A-1, a
structural rule; H2 (Q59) resolved the anchor it set out to resolve and did
not deliver the exact-answer figure it predicted.** With both changes on
(`anchor_first`), the exposed bank's T3 goes 0.3267 → 0.3465 exact,
0.6223 → 0.6736 recall, `wrong_entity` 0.0693 → 0.0495,
`catalogue_dump` 0.1287 → 0.0000, and the T3 questions reaching no anchor fall
from 37 to 4 of 101. T1/T2 are byte-identical to baseline. On the sealed bank
T3 goes 0.6500 → 0.6750 exact and 0.7812 → 0.8925 recall with zero wrong
entities and zero dumps. Every dump on every tier of both banks is gone; T6
(invented anchors) answers nothing wrong on either bank. One deterministic run
per arm; the rewrite model rung is the only model call and is identical
across arms.

The predicted T3 exact figure (≥ 0.45 exposed, ≥ +0.10 sealed) was **not**
reached: resolving the anchor on 97 of 101 questions turns into an exact
answer on only 6 more of them, because what happens *after* the anchor binds —
the deterministic rung rendering a describe or a wrong relation — is the next
defect (plan Steps 5–6), and this experiment measured it rather than fixed it.

## 4. Interpretation

**H1 is falsified.** The 1 % document-frequency cut lost the sealed T3 gold
bind on "Hardware" (1 of 40), cut sealed T4/T5 wrong binds by only
36 % / 43 % against the 60 % line, left 5 T6 binds against ≤ 2, and on
the exposed bank dropped "Phishing" and "APT29" — common words that are also
the names analysts use. It also cannot see the tool named "at", whose two-letter
name the tokenizer never indexes. ISSUES.md records that the review's 60/60
figure only counted T3 questions that bound uniquely.

**A-1 does what H1 was for.** A one-word name binds only when the question
writes it as a standalone proper noun — same casing, whole word, not inside a
longer capitalised run after the sentence-initial word, at least one indexable
token. No number to tune; nothing catalogue-specific. Probe level: exposed T3
gold binds 76 → 78 (no loss; ambiguities resolve), T4 wrong binds 28 → 10,
T5 26 → 6, T6 9 → 0; sealed T3 26 → 39 gold with no loss, T4 14 → 2,
T5 14 → 3, T6 15 → 3. The residue is multi-word real names inside invented
ones ("Template Injection" inside "Edge Template Injection") and acronyms the
question genuinely writes ("SSH", "VNC"). The sealed T6 line (≤ 2) is missed by
one; the T4/T5 reduction lines (≥ 60 %) are met on both banks; the T3 line (≤
2 % loss) is met with zero loss.

**A-1 unmasked two things, and this step fixed one.** With the false anchor
gone, T4/T5 questions fell to seedless relation templates that scan the
catalogue — sealed `catalogue_dump` rose to 0.2750 / 0.4750. The
unanchored-relation guard closes that: `hygiene` has 0.0500 / 0.0250 sealed and
0.0192 / 0.0096 exposed. The other is the rewrite stage's deterministic rung
rendering a describe or a card-vocabulary relation the question did not ask
for on three sealed T3 questions that now *reach* it ("attributed to Tonto
Team" → ATTRIBUTED_TO); that is Steps 5–6's defect and costs 3 sealed T3
exact against 1 fixed under `hygiene`.

**H2's mechanism holds; its exact-answer prediction does not.** Anchor-first
routing leaves 4 of 101 exposed T3 questions unresolved (from 37; predicted ≤ 15)
and 0 of 40 sealed. T1/T2 unchanged; T6 `wrong_entity` 0.0000; T4/T5 `wrong_entity` at
or below D-035 (0.1442 / 0.1154 against 0.1442 / 0.1346). But T3 `records_exact` is
0.3465 exposed (predicted ≥ 0.45) and 0.6750 sealed (predicted ≥ +0.10 over
0.6500). Paired against `hygiene`, anchor-first turns 15 exposed abstentions into
answers and fixes 6 against 3 broken; the answers that are not exact are the
relation, not the entity — recall rises 0.5460 → 0.6736 while wrong-entity
stays flat. The 0.45 prediction assumed a bound anchor becomes an exact answer;
it becomes a *correctly-anchored* answer, and exactness is the next step's job.

**What moves for the analyst.** Across both banks and every tier, the
combined arm is never worse than the shipped tree on `records_exact`, better
on recall and wrong-entity at T3, equal or better on wrong-entity at T4/T5,
and removes every catalogue dump. That is the not-worse-than-baseline rule
D-034 was shipped under; the ship decision itself belongs to plan Step 10.

## 5. Threats to validity

- A-1 was designed after the probe had run on both banks; its probe-level
  sealed figures are not blind. The harness-level sealed figures
  (`scored_hygiene.json`, `scored_anchor_first.json`) are the first
  measurement of it that was not visible during design. ISSUES.md.
- The exposed `distinct` run is timeout-contaminated and not reported; the
  `hygiene` and `anchor_first` arms carry 600 s budgets. ISSUES.md.
- The rewrite model rung ran uncached in the new arms and cached in the
  baseline; at temperature 0 the difference is latency, and no row in the
  reported arms carries the timeout signature.
- The sealed bank has no federated questions and its T3 shapes are simpler
  than the exposed bank's, which is why its baseline T3 is 0.65 against 0.33.
  Deltas are comparable across banks; levels are not.
- The runs were made from the working tree that commits `e055e2bb` and
  `a0888b33` then captured without further code change (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That the discriminating signal for "is this word a name" is not how rare the
word is but how the analyst *wrote* it. "Phishing" is one of the commonest
words in the corpus and one of the commonest anchors analysts ask for; no
frequency threshold separates it from "Credentials". Casing does — and it
cost nothing on either bank's T3.

And that the sealed bank's invented anchors bound a real node fifteen times
out of forty before the rule, because "Malware" is a sub-technique's name.

## 7. Follow-ups

- **Q59** is amended: the lookup order is fixed; the remaining T3 loss is the
  deterministic rung's rendering (plan Steps 5–6), measured here as the gap
  between anchors resolved (97/101) and answers exact (35/101).
- The three multi-word residues at T6 ("Template Injection" inside "Edge
  Template Injection") need a run-membership check for multi-word names, the
  same idea A-1 applies to one-word names; not done here because no line was
  pre-registered for it.
- Lower-cased single-word names ("what is phishing") no longer bind by exact
  match and fall to the closed-choice rung, which D-035 stops from choosing
  the anchor. Plan Step 9's disclosed acceptance is where that case belongs.
