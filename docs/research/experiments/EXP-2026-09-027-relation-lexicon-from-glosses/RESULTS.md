# RESULTS — EXP-2026-09-027 A relation lexicon generated from the card's own glosses, offline

## 1. Headline

**H1 is falsified at T1 and met in direction at T2/T3.** With the generated
lexicon read as gloss hints, exposed T2 `records_exact` rises 0.3667 → 0.4000
(4 fixed, 0 broken) and T3 0.3762 → 0.4059 (4 fixed, 1 broken), but T1
falls 0.3917 → 0.3583 (4 fixed, 8 broken) — below the not-worse-than-baseline line the
plan applies. Recall rises on all three tiers (+0.0368 / +0.0465 / +0.0083) with `wrong_entity`,
`catalogue_dump` and T6 unchanged; single-record answers on T1/T2 fall only
76 → 69 (predicted ≤ 20). The sealed bank moves by one T3 case. The lexicon is
not shipped as generated; the mechanism is portable and the words are not yet
good enough. One deterministic run per arm.

## 4. Interpretation

**The words bind, and they bind too widely.** MITIGATES gained "defend",
"protect against", "countermeasure", "safeguard"; USES gained "tool",
"tools", "capability", "operation", "employed", "leveraged". The second list
is the T1 loss: "tool" and "operation" occur in questions that are not about
USES, a gloss hit binds the relation, and a question the template cascade
answered as a describe or a different relation now runs USES. The filter
dropped words that *name a label* ("attack method") and words offered for two
relationships, but not ordinary nouns that merely co-occur with one
relationship's members.

**The gains are the words that are verbs.** T2's four fixes and T3's four are
"defend against" / "stop" / "spot" shapes binding MITIGATES and DETECTS. A
lexicon restricted to the generated *verbs and verb phrases* — a further
filter the protocol did not pre-register — is the obvious next arm and is not
run here.

**Portability position.** The generator ran on both cards from nothing but
their glosses and endpoints, dropped 2 of 30 relationships honestly, and the
consumer reads a sidecar keyed by dataset. What is not portable yet is the
quality of the model's word choice for noun-heavy relationships.

## 5. Threats to validity

- One generation at temperature 0; a different prompt version would produce
  different words, and the verdict is about *these* words.
- The lexicon was generated after the exposed bank had been read many times;
  no bank question was shown to the generator, but the prompt author had
  seen them.
- Run from the working tree later committed unchanged (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That the losing words are nouns, not verbs: "tool" costs more T1 answers than
"defend" gains.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Restrict the lexicon to verbs and verb phrases (part-of-speech from the
  model's own output, or a second prompt) and re-measure — a pre-registered
  next arm, not an adjustment of this one.
- Regenerate the two ungenerated CAPEC relationships with a larger budget.
