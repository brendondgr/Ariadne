**H1 holds in direction and misses its line; H2 is falsified.** Executing the
bound form directly lifts exposed T3 `records_exact` 0.3465 → 0.3762
(paired: 4 fixed, 1 broken; predicted ≥ + 0.05, measured + 0.0297) and recall

(2 fixed, 1 broken). Lifting the D-034 identifier skip on top (`form_execute_ids`)
drops T1 `records_exact` 0.3917 → 0.3000 and T2 0.3667 → 0.3167
(4+6 fixed against 15+12 broken), far below the control − 0.02 line: **the identifier
skip stays**. One deterministic run per arm; the rewrite model rung is the
only model call.

## 4. Interpretation

**The round trip was a loss, and a smaller one than predicted.** Direct
execution recovers five exposed T3 answers and costs one, all in the
population that reaches the deterministic rung with a bound relation; the
prediction of five *net* was optimistic by about half. T1/T2 are untouched
because the identifier skip keeps id-bearing questions off the stage, which
is exactly what the arm was meant to show.

**Lifting the identifier skip fails for the same reason it failed in
EXP-2026-09-021.** With the anchor held (D-035) and the form executed
directly, the model's *relation and target-label* choices on id-bearing
questions still break 27 answers to fix 10: recall rises
(+0.0377 / +0.0569) because the chosen relation is often a superset of what the
template cascade would have returned, and exactness falls because it is not
the same set. The template cascade's routing on an identifier plus a verb is
better than the model's closed choice of relation for these questions. So the
round trip was not what made the model rung unsafe there; the choice was.

**Mode from the target label (the disclosed amendment) is part of the gain.**
The sealed T3 fixes include the "Putter Panda" shape whose form previously
bound as a describe; the two sealed fixes and one break are recorded paired.

**Ship position.** `form_execute` is never worse than `anchor_first` on
`records_exact` on either bank and better on T3 recall; under the
not-worse-than-baseline rule it qualifies. `form_execute_ids` does not.

## 5. Threats to validity

- T4/T5 `wrong_entity` moves by one case each in opposite directions across
  the two banks; that is noise at n = 104 and 40 and is not claimed either way.
- The amendment (mode from a bound target label) was made from a three-question
  smoke test before any bank was scored; it is disclosed in PROTOCOL.md and its
  effect is not separated from direct execution's in these arms.
- Run from the working tree later committed unchanged (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That the model rung's harm on id-bearing questions survives removing the
render round trip. The measurement in EXP-2026-09-021 Step 4 attributed the
32 breaks partly to rendering; this arm says most of them were the relation
choice itself.

## 7. Follow-ups

- The relation the model chooses on an id-bearing question is a vocabulary
  problem (plan Step 8): with a card that carries "defend against" → MITIGATES
  the deterministic rung binds it and the model is never asked.
- D-034's identifier skip is re-affirmed; no decision entry is needed.
