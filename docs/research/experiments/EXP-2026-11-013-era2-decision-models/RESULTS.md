# RESULTS — EXP-2026-11-013 Era 2 Step 7: which closed decisions can small models own, by role

## 1. Headline

**No decision earns "alone".** Every decision now has a measured role for each of the three
fast models, against Qwen 3.8 27B choosing with reasoning.

- **Grounding (D2 + D3):** Qwen read through its option probabilities, with no reasoning, is a
  **gate**. It accepts 56% of items at 4.4% wrong, in transfer across 200 anchor splits.
- **Relation choice (D4) and the exit decision:** no fast model holds a gate.
- **The small tests** (capped at gate):
  - Graph choice (D1) and untrusted-text screening (D12) are nearly solved by `qwen_prob`
    and `pplx`, which meet every "alone" bound.
  - Sufficiency (D9) and question shape (D5) are gates for `pplx`.
  - The property filter (D6) is a gate for `d3`, on thin data.
  - Node kind (D7) and plan check (D8) have no usable fast model.

**The dominant failure, in every arm including the baseline, is "none of these".**
- When the right entity is among the options, Qwen without reasoning picks it exactly as
  often as with reasoning (0.94 and 0.94).
- When it is not, Qwen says so on 40% of items with no reasoning and 56% with reasoning.
- pplx-decider says so on 33% and d3-lite on 26%.

**Hypotheses** (§4):
- D2/D3 holds.
- The exit hypothesis holds, in a stronger form: even the baseline is unreliable.
- The D4 hypothesis is half falsified: the decision models are not competitive on the
  identifier and name tiers.
- The calibration-transfer hypothesis is falsified: the exact-name stratum is among those
  that transfer best.

## 4. Interpretation

### The hypotheses, one by one

**D4: decision models competitive on the identifier and name tiers, weak on synonyms. Half
falsified.**
- **Identifier and name tiers, items with 2+ options:**
  - pplx 0.68 and 0.75;
  - d3 0.58 and 0.66;
  - the baseline 0.97 and 0.98.
- **Neither is competitive.** By the falsifier, pplx fails the gate on the identifier tier
  (median transfer wrong 0.0625). It sits exactly on the bound for the name tier (0.05, p90
  0.17). Its D4 flip rate of 0.125 is above the gate's 0.10 on its own. d3 fails both tiers.
- **The weakness on synonyms holds for pplx only.** pplx falls to 0.52 on the synonym tier
  and `qwen_prob` to 0.76 (from 0.91 on identifiers). d3 is weak on every tier (0.45 to
  0.66) and no worse on synonyms (0.65). No model reaches "alone" on any tier.

**D2/D3: the LLM with reasoning beats the small models. Holds.**
- **Overall:** pplx 0.695 and d3 0.685 against 0.816, a gap of 0.12 to 0.13.
- **Same model, no reasoning:** `qwen_prob` comes within 0.048 (0.768). On the falsifier's
  wording ("a decision model") this does not count: it is the baseline model, not a decision
  model.
- **The gap is almost all "none of these".** On items where the right entity is offered,
  d3 (0.89) and pplx (0.87) are within 0.07 of the baseline. They miss the gold on 11–13% of
  those items, not the 36% the kill tests recorded for the decider alone.

**Exit decision: rules, not models, carry out of coverage. Holds, and is stronger than
stated.**
- **No arm is reliable, the baseline included** (0.69).
- **The baseline's errors.** It sends 33 of 51 ambiguous items to "answerable", and 17 of 53
  out-of-coverage items.
- **It misses path search and similarity.** It catches only 2 of 10 path-search questions
  and 1 of 10 similarity questions. A model reading the catalogue note does not see these as
  unsupported.
- **The fast models are biased:** d3 answers "out of coverage" for 59 of 85 answerable items.
- **For Step 8 and later:** out of coverage needs a rule that matches the asked operation
  against the catalogue. Ambiguity needs a check on the data (more than one non-empty
  reading), not a model's reading of the question.

**Calibration transfer holds on most strata and fails on exact-name questions. Falsified.**
- **The exact-name stratum is one of the best.** On grounding it transfers within every
  bound:
  - `qwen_prob`: coverage 0.97, median wrong 0.0, p90 0.03;
  - d3: 0.95, 0.029, 0.059.
- **Most other strata fail the p90 bound.** On D2+D3, only the identifier and name strata
  meet it, and only for `qwen_prob` and d3; pplx meets it on none. On D4, no stratum meets it
  at any coverage.
- **Caveat:** the strata hold 46–69 items, so each test half holds about 30, and a p90 over
  halves that small is noisy (§5).

### What it means for the agent (Steps 8 onward)

1. **Grounding:** `qwen_prob` can sit in front of the controller as a gate on the positive
   choice. "None of these" cannot be left to any model's top option. It needs its own
   signal, for example the name index's score gap or a separate yes/no check, and that is
   untested here (follow-up 1).
2. **Relation choice:** stays with the controller. The best fast model accepts 28% at 6.3%
   wrong, outside the 5% bound.
3. **Exit decision:** is rules first. Out of coverage is a catalogue match, and ambiguity a
   reading count. The model is a fallback only.
4. **Cheap checks** that the agent can take from fast models, behind a gate:
   - graph choice (D1);
   - untrusted-text screening (D12);
   - sufficiency (D9, pplx);
   - question shape (D5, pplx).
5. **Plan check (D8) and node kind (D7)** stay with the controller.
   - D8 has 30 "yes" and 90 "no" items, so answering "no" every time scores 0.75. Every arm
     is below that: the fast models at 0.43 to 0.55, and the baseline at 0.71.
   - D7 is balanced, and pplx (0.46) is below chance on it.
6. **An agreement vote is not a substitute for a threshold.** The fast models' errors are
   correlated.

**Claims:** none are moved. This is an engineering measurement for the era-2 design, not a
paper claim.

## 5. Threats to validity

- **One run, tuning split only.** The roles are estimates from the Sonnet-worded tuning
  banks. The sealed held-out banks were not read, and the thresholds should be re-fitted
  before the agent is scored on them.
- **Small cells.** The Phase C tests have 60 to 150 items.
  - D5 has 2 scan and 2 other questions, and D6 has 5 or fewer per CAPEC property.
  - Per-stratum transfer works on halves of about 30 items.
  - A "gate" from these is a lead, which is why Phase C is capped there.
- **The items are constructed.**
  - **Gold is injected.** D2 puts the gold into the candidates when BM25 misses it (recall@8
    is 0.72), so grounding is scored with upstream retrieval made perfect.
  - **D3's replacement is hard.** D3 swaps in the ninth search result, a near neighbour, so
    "none" items are harder than a random distractor.
  - **D8's items are synthetic.** D8's wrong plans are single edits (another relation, a
    reversed direction, another anchor), not real planner errors.
  - **The binary sets are unbalanced.** D8 (30 yes, 90 no) and D9 (40 yes, 80 no) are
    unbalanced, so their accuracies must be read against an always-"no" answer (0.75 and
    0.67).
- **The exit boundary is the catalogue note's wording.** The note lists "repeat along
  further named relations". A path-search question can be read as exactly that, so some of
  the misses on path search may be a fair reading of an unclear note.
- **The baseline and `qwen_prob` are the same model.** Their agreement is not independent
  evidence. The baseline is also not gold: it scores 0.69 to 0.99 depending on the decision.
- **The interfaces differ by arm.**
  - pplx gets opaque keys (`c1..cN`).
  - The other arms get letters.
  - `qwen_prob` reads probabilities from the top 20 log-probabilities, so a letter outside
    them counts as 0.
  - `qwen_prob`'s flip rates (0.10 to 0.24) suggest a letter-position bias that the opaque
    keys avoid.
- **A shared server.** Run-log times include queueing (ISSUES). §2.6 is from a separate
  sequential probe, under the load of the moment.

## 6. What surprised us

- **Reasoning buys "none of these", and almost nothing else on grounding.** With the right
  entity offered, Qwen without reasoning matches Qwen with reasoning exactly (0.9403 and
  0.9403). The reasoning path, about 350 generated tokens against one, earns its cost only when the
  gold is absent.
- **The baseline is a poor "none" detector too.** On relation choice it says "none" for
  only a third of the items where it should (precision 1.0, recall 0.33). It prefers a
  plausible wrong relation to admitting none fits.
- **Three models agreeing is worse than one model with a threshold.** On D4 and D8 the
  agreement gate's wrong rate is 23% and 52%. Small models share failure modes.
- **D1 and D12 are not hard.** Graph choice and the injection screen are at 0.97 to 1.0 for
  two of the three fast models, with good calibration. These are the safest early wins for
  a fast path.
- **d3-lite has a constant bias on the exit decision.** It answers "out of coverage" for
  almost anything. That gives perfect recall on out of coverage and is useless as a
  decision.

## 7. Follow-ups

- **Q62.** "None of these" needs its own signal. Every arm, the baseline included, misses it
  on 44–74% of grounding items and 67–88% of relation items. Test a dedicated yes/no check
  ("is the referent among these?") and the name index's score gap as a gate in front of the
  choice.
- **Q63.** The exit decision needs rules before a model:
  - an operation-against-catalogue match for out of coverage (path search and similarity
    are missed);
  - a non-empty-reading count for ambiguity.

  Then re-measure what is left for the model.

Both are in `../../OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`.

## 8. Reproduction

```bash
E=docs/research/experiments/EXP-2026-11-013-era2-decision-models
uv run python scripts/era2_decisions.py build --phase A --out $E/data   # then --phase B, --phase C
uv run python scripts/era2_decisions.py run --data $E/data --arms qwen_prob,pplx,d3,qwen_reason
uv run python scripts/era2_decisions.py latency --data $E/data --arms qwen_prob,pplx,d3,qwen_reason
uv run python scripts/era2_decisions.py score --data $E/data
```

**What a re-run needs:**
- **Servers:** the relay on `:9090` serving Qwen 3.8 27B, pplx-decider on `:7500`, and
  d3-lite on `:5107`.
- **Backends:** the ATT&CK and CAPEC backends loaded, for the build.
- **Resuming:** runs append to `data/runs/<arm>.jsonl` and skip what is already there.
