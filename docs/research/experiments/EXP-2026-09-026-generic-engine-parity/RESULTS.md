# RESULTS — EXP-2026-09-026 Generic-engine parity on the named defects

## 1. Headline

**H1 is falsified: the schema-derived engine does not reach parity with the
template cascade, on either bank, before or after the two dispatch fixes.**
On the exposed bank the first `generic` arm lost 17/12/28 answers at T1/T2/T3 against
0/1/2 fixed; after A-1 (the reverse bridge lands on the join-owner label; the
engine is fed by the executed form) `generic_form` against `form_execute` still
loses 11/6/14 against 0/1/0 fixed, and 18 of 40 sealed T3. What A-1 did
close is the reverse-mapping category (REV: 16 template-only cases → 2, 14 now
answered by both). Recall is at parity where exact is not (exposed T3
0.7042 templates vs 0.7012 generic): the engine returns wider
neighbourhoods than the cascade's shaped queries. The templates are not
retired. One deterministic run per arm.

## 4. Interpretation

**Two of the three named defects were real and are fixed.** A joined
sub-question whose seeds belong to no label of its source now takes the
bridge's reverse shape (the trace of "which CAPEC items line up with T1040"
bound an ATT&CK id as a CAPEC id and was rejected), and the reverse walk lands
on the label that owns the mapping edge instead of whatever the plan named
(`AlternateTerm`, matching nothing). REV went from 16 template-only cases to
2. A joined sub-question naming no native relation now bridges too, which is
what "correspond to" and "linked to" needed.

**The remaining gap is not dispatch, it is shape.** The cascade's templates
return exactly the rows a question shape asks for; the generic walk returns
every declared path to every named label and the chain binds every entity
the router matched. Where the gold is a shaped subset — a group's techniques
rolled up to parents, a tactic's techniques without their sub-techniques, a
software's techniques on one platform — the generic answer is a superset:
recall holds, exact fails. MAP (9), CWE (5), GRP (4), TAC/SW/CTT (3 each) are
of this kind. Closing them means giving the generic engine the cascade's
shaping rules — projection, roll-up, filter — as declared, card-level
operations, which is plan Step 6's form with filters and is not this step.

**Portability position.** The engine that runs on cards alone answers T3 at
0.24 exact / 0.70 recall on the exposed bank and 0.25 / 0.70 sealed, against
the cascade's 0.38 / 0.70 and 0.70 / 0.93. A new graph gets the recall; it
does not yet get the exactness.

## 5. Threats to validity

- A-1 was designed after the first arm was scored and re-run on the same
  banks; it is labelled as such in PROTOCOL.md.
- The category table splits on the case id's middle token, which is the bank
  authors' category, not a property of the question.
- Run from the working tree later committed unchanged (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That one wrong label in a query the engine was already choosing correctly
(`AlternateTerm` where the registry says `AttackPattern`) was the whole of
the largest losing category.

## 7. Follow-ups

- Declared shaping operations for the generic engine (roll-up, filter,
  projection) as form fields — plan Step 6's form, extended.
- MAP's residual: the ATT&CK consumer's generic chain returns tactics and
  sub-techniques beside the mapped techniques; a consumer seeded through a
  join should answer the join's label only.
