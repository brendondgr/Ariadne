**H1 is falsified on its exact-match guard and confirms a modest recall gain.**

and T2 0.4838 → 0.5301 (predicted ≥ 0.75), with `catalogue_dump` and
`wrong_entity` unchanged on every tier — and lowers T1/T2 `records_exact`
0.3917 → 0.3000 and 0.3667 → 0.2417 (guard: ± 0.02). Paired, it fixes
nothing and widens 11 + 15 answers whose gold is the anchor's own single record.

there lacks a relation word. One deterministic run per arm.

## 4. Interpretation

The fallback fires exactly where designed — an anchored, non-definitional
question none of whose words match a facet — and on the exposed bank that
population contains two kinds of question the policy cannot tell apart:

- **relation gaps** ("T1047 — what stops it?", "How would I spot T1566
  occurring?"), where the gold is a relation's members and the shipped tree
  returned one record: 7 T1 and 7 T2 questions gain ≥ 0.1 recall, several to
  1.0 ("How would I spot T1566" goes 0.125 → 1.0);
- **describe-shaped golds** ("T1021.001: what do we call it?", "Which assets
  does T1047 affect?" — whose gold is the technique itself because ICS assets
  are not loaded), where any expansion is a superset and `records_exact`
  turns false: 11 T1 and 15 T2 questions.

The deterministic intent classifier does not separate them: every recall gain
is on an `id`-cited question the classifier calls a lookup, and 10 of the 26
widenings are too. There is no gate short of reading the analyst's mind.

**The pre-registered exact guard was inconsistent with the mechanism.** A
superset of a one-record gold is not exact by definition; the guard could
only pass if the fallback never fired on such a question. That is recorded
here as a protocol flaw rather than moved after the fact.

`abstained` rises 0.1917 → 0.2667 at T1 because the harness marks an answer
abstained when *any* sub-question abstains, and an expansion hop into a joined
source can abstain while the first hop answered ("How would I spot T1566"
reaches recall 1.0 and is counted abstained). That is a reporting artefact of
multi-hop answers, not lost answers, and is noted for Step 10's judged pass.

**Ship position.** Under `records_exact` the arm loses; under the plan's
stated post-Step-5 rule (recall primary, dump and wrong-entity as guards) it
gains modestly with both guards flat. The flag stays off until Step 10 decides
on the judged pass; it is not turned on here.

## 5. Threats to validity

- Recall here is over a 1-record gold on many T1/T2 questions, so a widened
  answer keeps recall 1.0 and loses exactness; neither metric says whether
  the extra records help the analyst. The judged pass is the arbiter.
- The 50-member facet cap excludes the very facets that carry some gold
  ("What methods does G0016 rely on?": the group's technique facet is over
  the cap, recall unchanged). Recorded, not re-run with a larger cap.
- Run from the working tree later committed unchanged (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That a fifth of the exposed bank's T1/T2 questions have a one-record gold —
the describe — under wording that is not definitional. The bank measures
"did you return exactly the describe" on questions an analyst would read as
asking for the technique's neighbourhood.

## 7. Follow-ups

- A judged pass on the `expand_unbound` answers (plan Step 10) is the only
  way to score a widened answer fairly.
- The harness's `abstained` should distinguish a whole-answer abstention from
  an abstaining expansion hop.
