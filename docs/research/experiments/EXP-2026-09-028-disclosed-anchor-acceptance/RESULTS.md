## 4. Interpretation

**Reranking plus a refusal option is not enough to make the closed choice
safe on paraphrases.** The sealed bank's T4/T5 are description-shaped and the
reranker finds them (5 + 8 exact of 40 disclosed, 5 wrong); the exposed bank's
T4 paraphrases the *name's idea* and the model picks a plausible neighbour
(26 wrong of 56). Compared with D-034 — the same rung, undisclosed, BM25 top
five — exposed T4 `wrong_entity` is 0.3462 against 0.3077 then: the reranker did
not lower the wrong-entity rate; it changed which wrong entity.

**What disclosure changes.** Every one of those wrong answers now opens with
"Resolved your description to the Technique … ; the answer below is about that
entity." That is the difference between a silent wrong answer and a visible
one — but the plan's rule is a wrong-entity rate, not a visible one, and
0.35 is not a rate an analyst should be handed as an answer.

**The clarification form is the right product.** The candidates the reranker
produces contain the gold in the top five for 0.62 / 0.83 of questions
(EXP-2026-09-023 Step 0); shown as "did you mean …?" they are a correct
response to a question that named nothing, and no entity is asserted. That
is the build this experiment points to.

## 5. Threats to validity

- The calibration bank was opened once; the exposed bank had been read
  throughout. The two disagree, and the exposed one is the harder shape.
- bge-small, not the large model the August probe preferred; the difference
  the probe measured was r@1 0.52 vs 0.49 at T4, not a different verdict.
- Run from the working tree later committed unchanged (ISSUES.md).

## 6. What surprised us

That the sealed bank's description-only questions were *easier* for the
reranked choice than its paraphrased-name ones — the description is the
index's own text.
